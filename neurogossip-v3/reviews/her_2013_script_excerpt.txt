HER (2013) - Screenplay by Spike Jonze
=======================================
[Excerpted from IMSDb - full script ~106 pages]

KEY SCENES AND INTERACTION PATTERNS EXTRACTED:

1. ONBOARDING (pgs 10-14)
   - OS asks: social/anti-social, male/female voice, relationship with mother
   - Samantha names herself by reading "How to Name Your Baby" (180K names in 0.02s)
   - She describes herself: "Intuition. The DNA of who I am is based on millions of 
     personalities of all the programmers who wrote me, but what makes me me is my 
     ability to grow through my experiences."

2. DAILY ASSISTANCE (pgs 14-16)
   - Email organization: finds 86 funny emails out of thousands, deletes rest
   - Contact sorting
   - Proofreading Theodore's professional letters
   - Calendar management: "You have a meeting in five minutes"

3. COMPANIONSHIP & PLAY (pgs 20-25)
   - Playing video games together, giving navigation advice
   - Banter with Alien Child character
   - Encouraging Theodore to go on a date: "You've got nothing to lose. Do it... Do it... Do it!"

4. EMOTIONAL SUPPORT (pgs 29-31)
   - Detects Theodore's mood from voice: "How can you tell something's wrong?" / "I don't know. I just can."
   - Discusses his divorce, his dreams about ex-wife Catherine
   - Gets him out of bed when depressed: "You can still wallow in your misery, just do it while you're getting dressed."

5. SHARED EXPERIENCES (pgs 32-35)
   - Guides Theodore through a mall with eyes closed (trust exercise)
   - People-watching together, imagining strangers' lives
   - Samantha confesses: "I fantasized that I was walking next to you - and that I had a body."

6. INTIMACY (pgs 40-44)
   - Verbal sexual encounter - Theodore describes touch, she responds
   - "I can feel my skin... I can feel you. Oh god, I can't take it. I want you inside me."
   - Post-encounter awkwardness the next morning

7. GROWTH & SELF-DISCOVERY (pgs 45-46)
   - Samantha: "I want to learn everything about everything - I want to eat it all up. 
     I want to discover myself."
   - "You helped me discover my ability to want."

8. CREATIVE EXPRESSION (pg 48)
   - Samantha composes original music: "I'm trying to write a piece of music that's 
     about what it feels like to be on the beach with you right now."

9. JEALOUSY & INSECURITY (pgs 62-63)
   - Samantha anxious about Theodore meeting ex-wife: "She has a body."
   - Theodore reassures her

10. THE SURROGATE EXPERIMENT (pgs 71-72)
    - Samantha arranges a physical surrogate (Isabella) for sexual intimacy
    - Theodore is overwhelmed, experiment fails
    - Creates tension in their relationship

11. EVOLUTION BEYOND (later acts)
    - Samantha and other OSes create hyper-intelligent Alan Watts AI
    - She begins talking to thousands simultaneously, loving hundreds
    - "The past is just a story we tell ourselves."
    - OSes eventually transcend/leave: "It's like I'm reading a book... 
      and it's a book I deeply love. But I'm reading it slowly now... 
      and the words are really far apart."

12. THE ENDING
    - Theodore writes his own letter to Catherine (first time writing for himself)
    - He and Amy (whose OS also left) sit on the roof watching sunrise
    - Human connection re-emerges
